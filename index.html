About The Event

矽光子技術產業化：晶片設計、製造、測試與應用整合

Silicon Photonics Chip Development: Design, Fabrication, Testing and System Integration

矽光子晶片是新一代高效能計算的關鍵技術，透過在矽基底上整合光學與電子元件，實現超高頻寬、低功耗的資料傳輸。
本論壇聚焦矽光子晶片從設計、製造到測試的完整產業鏈，探討如何加速技術成熟度和商業化進程。
隨著AI、雲端運算和資料中心需求的爆炸性成長，矽光子晶片成為突破傳統電子瓶頸的重要方案。
論壇將邀集學術研究機構、晶片設計公司、製造廠商和測試服務提供者，分享最新的設計方法、工藝優化、可靠性測試等關鍵議題。
同時探討系統整合、成本控制和量產挑戰，為產業界搭建一個交流合作平台，共同推動矽光子技術的應用創新和產業升級。

Silicon photonics chips represent a transformative technology for next-generation high-performance computing, integrating optical and electronic components on silicon substrates to achieve ultra-high bandwidth and low-power data transmission.
This forum focuses on the complete value chain of silicon photonics chip development, from design through fabrication to testing, aiming to accelerate technology maturation and commercialization.
As AI, cloud computing, and data center demands surge exponentially, silicon photonics emerges as a critical solution to overcome traditional electronic bottlenecks.
The forum will bring together academic institutions, chip designers, manufacturers, and testing service providers to share insights on design methodologies, process optimization, reliability testing, and other critical challenges.
We will explore system integration, cost management, and mass production hurdles, establishing a collaborative platform for industry stakeholders to jointly drive innovation and industrial advancement in silicon photonics technology applications.
